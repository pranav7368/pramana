Claims are rejected if submitted more than 30 days after the discharge date. Rejected claims may be appealed within 60 days of the rejection notice. An appeal requires the original claim number and the rejection letter.

Maternity benefits are not available during the first policy year. Policy PX-4471 covers outpatient dental treatment up to Rs. 25,000 per year.

Employees may carry forward up to 15 unused leave days into the next year. Reimbursement requests must include original receipts and are settled within 21 working days.
